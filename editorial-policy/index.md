---
title: Editorial Policy
description: How this site protects technical accuracy and transparency.
permalink: /editorial-policy/
---

We do not publish fabricated test data, customer names, certifications, production cases, or commercial relationships. Relevant commercial relationships must be disclosed.

Parameters should link to an original source and state method and conditions. Typical values are not design guarantees. Illustrative calculations are labeled and kept separate from measurements.

Third-party brands are discussed objectively. Similar headline properties do not justify equivalence or superiority. Corrections update the visible date.
