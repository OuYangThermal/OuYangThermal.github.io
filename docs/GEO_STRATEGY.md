# OUYANG THERMAL GEO/SEO Strategy

## Objective

Build discoverability and technical trust that can convert qualified overseas engineering and procurement needs into private discussions, benchmarks, samples, qualification projects, RFQs and orders. Traffic volume alone is not the goal.

## Core rule

**Keyword Research First. Content Second.**

Do not publish random articles, chase a daily quota, or create three weak pages because an automation expected three. One differentiated, evidence-based page is better than three overlapping pages.

Keyword value is assessed through:

```text
Search Demand
× Commercial Intent
× Product Fit
× Application Fit
× Engineer Decision Value
× Ranking Opportunity
× AI Citation Potential
```

The current 100-point model uses: Commercial Intent 25; Product Fit 15; Application Fit 15; Engineer Decision Value 10; Search Demand Evidence 20; Ranking Opportunity 10; AI/GEO Citation Potential 5.

## Priority intent families

Prioritize real engineering questions, application selection and late-funnel language: supplier, manufacturer, China, alternative, replacement, second source, benchmark, qualification, sample and RFQ. Engineering modifiers include thickness, gap, compression, compression set, hardness, dielectric, thermal resistance, pump-out, void, dispensing and reliability.

Do not treat a professionally worded long sentence as a search query without evidence. Decompose it into shorter expressions that real buyers or engineers may use.

## Tier policy

- **Tier A:** real search-language evidence plus high commercial value; publish or update first after overlap review.
- **Tier B:** demand unknown or small but very high commercial value; precise supporting content, clearly labeled honestly.
- **Tier C:** observable demand but lower commercial value; use selectively for topic authority.
- **Tier D:** low/unsupported demand and low commercial value; do not produce now.

Autocomplete validates language, not monthly volume. Public result types validate that the intent exists, not how often it is searched. Do not invent Keyword Planner or Search Console data.

## Content decision workflow

1. Select a mapped keyword with evidence and commercial rationale.
2. Search all 30 articles, formal pages, titles, headings and internal links.
3. Read the keyword-map `Existing URL` and status fields.
4. Decide `UPDATE`, `CREATE`, or `SKIP`.
5. Prefer `UPDATE` when an existing page satisfies the same primary intent.
6. For `CREATE`, document the distinct intent and reciprocal internal-link plan.
7. Prepare a Keyword Brief: primary keyword, evidence, intent, score, existing URL, action, supporting terms, questions, links, persona and CTA.
8. Draft with Answer First, engineering explanation, selection logic, parameters/units, common mistakes, validation, FAQ, natural links and restrained CTA.
9. Human technical and commercial review.
10. Build and test. Publish only after explicit approval during the controlled pilot.

## Editorial integrity

Never invent tests, numerical performance, customer cases, certifications, rankings, search volume, supplier relationships or manufacturing ownership. Label calculations as illustrations and provide conditions. Cite primary standards and original technical sources where possible. State uncertainty and distinguish supplier typical data from guaranteed application performance.

Commercial pages should naturally offer: Discuss Your Application, Benchmark Your Current TIM, and Request a Sample. WhatsApp, email and call remain available without forcing the form.

## AI/GEO usefulness

Write self-contained answers that define terms, expose assumptions, use correct units, distinguish related concepts and provide verification steps. This improves usefulness to engineers and makes passages easier to quote accurately. Do not optimize for artificial “AI scores” at the expense of accuracy or clarity.
