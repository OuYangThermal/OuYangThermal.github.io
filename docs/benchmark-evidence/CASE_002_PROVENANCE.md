# Case 002 provenance — FT-BN050

## Data source

Case 002 was prepared from a user-supplied material pack containing a public-data summary and three source-evidence JPEGs. The JPEGs were used only to verify the displayed method, conditions and numerical tables. They are excluded from the repository and public site.

## Evidence mapping

- ASTM D5470: five FT-BN050 nominal/compressed thickness pairs and measured thermal-resistance values at 50 psi.
- ASTM D149: five displayed breakdown-voltage limits for a stated 200 × 200 mm test area.
- Environment: 25 ± 3 °C and 65 ± 10% RH.
- Sample identifier and color: FT-BN050, white.

The report section heading immediately above the raw thermal-result images contains an inconsistent `FT-BN035` label. The public summary, main data table and individual raw-result captions identify the sample as FT-BN050. The inconsistent heading is treated as a source-document typographical error and is not reproduced publicly.

## Anonymization

No source screenshot is published. Company, department, operator, equipment/software identifiers, report ownership language, dates and device photographs are excluded. Public visuals are newly drawn from the verified numerical table and contain no source branding or personal information.

## Derived assets

- Thermal resistance versus nominal thickness chart.
- Nominal/compressed thickness and thermal-resistance selection guide.
- ASTM D149 electrical-insulation summary.

Derived graphics preserve displayed numerical values and greater-than limits. They do not infer thermal conductivity, device temperature or unreported performance.

## Limitations and publication decisions

- Evidence is internal testing, not independent third-party certification.
- Results apply only to the stated samples and test conditions.
- No customer endorsement, field reliability, certification or production approval is claimed.
- The series does not establish a thermal-conductivity value.
- Application decisions still require assembly-specific thermal, mechanical, electrical and reliability validation.
