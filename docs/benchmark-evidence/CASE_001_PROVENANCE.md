# Case 001 provenance record

## Publication decision

- Evidence type: internal comparative test data.
- Public sample name permitted: FT-BN035.
- Comparative identification: SP2000 reference sample.
- The reference result represents only the specific sample tested under the stated conditions.
- The evidence is not an independent, third-party, official or manufacturer certification.
- Search demand for the proposed primary phrase remains Unknown.

## Source-to-data mapping

The supplied evidence package contained five source images: two raw ASTM D5470 screens and three report extracts covering the formal thermal-resistance series, electrical results, surface resistance and density. Every public numerical value was reconciled visually against those images.

The formal ASTM D5470 table supports five FT-BN035 results and one 0.25 mm SP2000 reference-sample result. The report states approximately 50 psi for the formal series. The separate raw screen around 0.22 mm, 2.03 W/m·K, 0.168 °C·in²/W and 49.9 psi is explicitly excluded from the formal series.

ASTM D149 supports the displayed breakdown-voltage values. ASTM D257 supports approximately 1.62 × 10^14 Ω at 500 V. ASTM D792 supports the three density measurements and the rounded average.

## Anonymization

Unredacted source screenshots are excluded from Git and from the public site. They contain internal personnel, laboratory, equipment, software, date or approval information. Public derivatives use clean reconstructed graphics. The single raw-test derivative is cropped and covers identifying header information without changing the displayed measurement values.

## Derived assets

1. Thermal resistance versus thickness chart.
2. 0.25 mm same-nominal-thickness comparison card.
3. Electrical-property summary.
4. Test-method evidence map.

All derivatives are 1440 × 900 WebP files. They state that results depend on samples and test conditions. The raw derivative is not used as a formal-series data source.

## Limitations and wording controls

- Use “approximately 21% lower measured thermal resistance under this specific internal test condition.”
- Never use “21% better,” universal superiority, guaranteed replacement or manufacturer-certified comparison.
- Do not imply that the reference result represents every SP2000 lot or product.
- Do not derive a formal FT-BN035 specification from the separate raw run.
- Customer qualification, multiple lots, application pressure, contact, reliability and assembly validation remain required.
- SP2000 is referenced solely for comparative identification; OUYANG THERMAL does not claim affiliation or endorsement.
