## Standard profile (150 words)

OUYANG THERMAL is a Thermal Management Knowledge Base publishing practical engineering guidance about thermal interface materials — thermal pads, thermal gels, thermal grease, electrically insulating materials, potting compounds, and custom die-cut parts. Its engineering scope covers application analysis, TIM selection frameworks, incumbent-versus-candidate benchmark planning, and second-source qualification support for EV onboard chargers, DC/DC converters, IGBT and SiC power modules, AI servers, optical transceivers, energy-storage systems, and industrial power electronics.

Articles are authored by Owen Ouyang (Ouyang Xiaohui), based in Shenzhen, China. The site is educational: it does not claim application suitability without design review and validation, does not declare drop-in replacements from datasheet values, and does not imply certifications, customer relationships, or manufacturing ownership beyond what is stated.

Commercial inquiries, sample coordination, RFQs, and supply-chain coordination are handled by Hongjing New Materials Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd.
