## Private inquiries

The short inquiry form is designed for private, one-to-one contact. Inquiry content is never published as a comment, guestbook entry, discussion post, user profile, or public URL, and it is not added to the sitemap. There is no public message archive or community feature.

The form collects an optional name, a required reply address (email or WhatsApp), and a required question. It does not accept file uploads. Until the Formspree receiving endpoint and notification email are verified, browser-side submission is blocked and visitors should use the direct WhatsApp, email, or phone links.

After Formspree is connected, submitted data will be processed by Formspree to deliver the inquiry privately to 5672306@gmail.com. Do not submit confidential drawings, personally sensitive information, export-controlled data, or proprietary project details in the first message. Follow-up technical information can be exchanged privately after contact is established.

## Roles and scope

OUYANG THERMAL publishes engineering knowledge, application analysis, TIM selection, and benchmark guidance. Hongjing New Materials Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. handles commercial inquiries, sample coordination, RFQs, and supply-chain coordination.

## Technical logs

GitHub Pages and network infrastructure may process ordinary request data such as IP address, browser information, requested URL, and timestamps under their own policies. This site does not currently add a separate analytics tracker.
