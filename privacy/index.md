## Current form status

The engineering inquiry forms on this website currently provide browser-side guidance and validation only. No form receiver, CRM, email service, database, analytics service, or file-upload service is connected. Clicking the review button does not transmit or store the information entered.

Do not submit confidential drawings, personally sensitive information, export-controlled data, or proprietary project details. If a receiving service is connected later, this notice will be updated before collection begins to identify the operator, data received, purpose, retention, processors, cross-border handling where applicable, and contact route for privacy requests.

## Roles and scope

OUYANG THERMAL publishes engineering knowledge, application analysis, TIM selection, and benchmark guidance. Hongjing New Materials Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. handles commercial inquiries, sample coordination, RFQs, and supply-chain coordination when a receiving channel is formally enabled.

## Technical logs

GitHub Pages and network infrastructure may process ordinary request data such as IP address, browser information, requested URL, and timestamps under their own policies. This site does not currently add a separate analytics tracker.
