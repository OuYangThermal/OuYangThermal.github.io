title: Methodology
description: How this site compares TIMs and interprets data.
permalink: /methodology/
---

Our sequence is: define the heat path; quantify geometry and tolerance; set thermal, electrical, and mechanical limits; document manufacturing constraints; compare aligned evidence; then validate in representative hardware.

We distinguish bulk conductivity from interface resistance. In an ideal slab, R = t/(kA), but real interfaces add contact and spreading effects. We prefer impedance at relevant thickness and pressure with method and uncertainty stated.

ASTM D5470 and ISO 22007-2 are not interchangeable; each has its own scope and assumptions.
