## Answer first

Internal comparative testing under the stated conditions measured the 0.25 mm FT-BN035 sample at **0.211 °C·in²/W**, compared with **0.267 °C·in²/W** for the specific tested 0.25 mm SP2000 reference sample. The FT-BN035 result was approximately 21% lower in this comparison. Thermal resistance was measured using ASTM D5470 at approximately 50 psi. Results are specific to the tested samples, pressure, thickness and conditions and do not establish universal product superiority. This is not an independent third-party certification.

## Surface resistance

The FT-BN035 source displays an ASTM D257 surface resistance of approximately **1.62 × 10¹⁴ Ω** at a test voltage of **500 V**. This result characterizes the tested specimen under the reported method; it does not replace system electrical-safety validation.

## Density

The ASTM D792 results displayed for FT-BN035 are **1.833, 1.875 and 1.900 g/cm³**, with a reported average of approximately **1.87 g/cm³**. Density is useful for material identification and consistency checks, but it does not establish thermal or electrical suitability by itself.

## Engineering interpretation

Thermal conductivity describes a material property under a stated method; thermal resistance describes resistance through a particular tested interface and thickness. Increasing thickness generally lengthens the heat path, which can increase the bulk resistance contribution. Actual results may also include contact resistance associated with surface flatness, wetting, pressure and fixture conditions.

That is why the same nominal thickness and approximately aligned pressure provide a more useful comparison than unrelated conductivity values. Even then, the candidate must be validated at the target bond-line thickness, mounting pressure, surface condition and application temperature. See [Thermal Conductivity vs Thermal Resistance]({{ '/comparison/thermal-conductivity-vs-thermal-resistance/' | relative_url }}) and [Why the Same Thermal Material Can Produce Different Test Results]({{ '/testing/why-can-the-same-thermal-material-produce-different-test-results/' | relative_url }}).
