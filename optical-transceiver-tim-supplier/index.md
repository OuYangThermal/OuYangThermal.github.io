## Direct answer

TIM selection for 400G, 800G, and 1.6T optical modules must balance case temperature, thin interfaces, tolerance, contact pressure, mechanical stress, assembly yield, rework, and reliability. OUYANG THERMAL supports engineering evaluation and Hongjing coordinates commercial inquiries and sample discussions.

## Key selection parameters

Define module architecture, heat-source location, interface dimensions, nominal and worst-case gap, clamping scheme, package stress limits, conductivity and resistance targets, electrical requirements, allowable silicone or volatile content where relevant, temperature range, and service expectations.

## Benchmark and qualification

Compare candidates at representative thickness and pressure. Check thickness tolerance, hardness, compression set, surface conformity, handling, contamination controls, and aged performance. Sample evaluation should use production-representative fixtures and documented acceptance criteria. Qualification remains the customer’s controlled process.
