What this tool does

The selector weighs interface gap, tolerance, electrical role and rework needs to suggest a material category, not a product. Thin, controlled interfaces may favor grease or a purpose-designed insulator; larger or variable gaps often favor pads or dispensable gels; permanent filling of complex volumes may require potting or encapsulation.

How to use the result

Treat the result as a shortlist. Confirm actual BLT, compression or dispensing behavior, contact resistance, dielectric requirements, operating environment, aging and serviceability. For example, a variable 1–3 mm electrically isolated interface may screen toward a thermal pad, but force limits and tolerance extremes still decide whether it can be assembled safely.

Engineering limitations

Final selection depends on geometry, assembly pressure, tolerance stack, surface condition, electrical safety, temperature, vibration, thermal cycling, service life and manufacturing process. No selection here represents a specific model recommendation or qualification result.
