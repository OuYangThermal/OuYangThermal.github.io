Answer first

Compare two TIM datasheets field by field, expose missing information and generate a controlled benchmark checklist. Higher W/m·K does not automatically mean lower total interface thermal resistance.

Comparison warning

Two TDS values are not directly comparable when test methods, BLT, pressure or specimen conditions differ.

What this tool calculates

The tool aligns conductivity, thickness, hardness, insulation, breakdown voltage, temperature range, reported thermal resistance and test method. When conductivity, thickness and area are all available, it calculates theoretical bulk resistance using R = t/(k × A). It does not calculate contact or system resistance.

How to use it

Transcribe only values stated in each supplier TDS and preserve their units and methods. A useful comparison begins by identifying missing or non-comparable fields; it does not award a winner from one conductivity number.

Example and engineering limitations

If Material A lists higher conductivity but uses a thicker installed BLT than Material B, its theoretical bulk resistance may still be higher. Real results also depend on wetting, compression, pressure, flatness, aging and test fixtures. Confirm candidates on the same substrate, BLT, mounting pressure and thermal method.
