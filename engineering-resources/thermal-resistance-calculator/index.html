This calculator estimates only the theoretical bulk thermal resistance of a uniform TIM layer using R = t/(kA). It does not include contact resistance, spreading resistance or cooling-system boundaries.

Enter positive values to calculate R in K/W.
## Formula and units `Rbulk = t / (k × A)` - Convert thickness `t` to metres. - Use conductivity `k` in W/m·K. - Convert contact area `A` to m². - The result is K/W for the defined uniform layer and area. ## Engineering limitation Actual assembly performance is also affected by **contact resistance, compression, surface roughness and flatness, assembly pressure, voids, spreading resistance, material aging and cooling boundaries**. Datasheet conductivity may also depend on the test method and condition. Use this result for first-order screening, then compare candidates at representative BLT, pressure, surfaces and temperature.
