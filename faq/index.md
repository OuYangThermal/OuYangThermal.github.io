## What is the most important TIM parameter?

There is no single universal parameter. Evaluate installed thermal impedance, thickness, contact, mechanical load, electrical needs, reliability, and process together.

## Does higher conductivity always lower component temperature?

No. The improvement depends on the interface share of total resistance and whether thickness, contact, spreading, and cooling remain equal.

## Can a datasheet replace application testing?

No. Datasheets support screening; final validation needs representative geometry, mounting, power, environment, and aging.

## Is thermal gel also a structural adhesive?

Usually not. Assign structural loads only when the supplier defines and qualifies that function.

## Which standard is common for TIM testing?

[ASTM D5470](https://store.astm.org/standards/d5470) covers thermal impedance and apparent conductivity for thermally conductive electrical insulation materials under defined conditions.
