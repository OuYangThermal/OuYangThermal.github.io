## Executive findings

The original commercial long-tail phrases are useful as page angles, but many are too long to treat as proven primary search queries. The validated map now separates a shorter **Primary Search Keyword** from the **Commercial Long-Tail Keyword**, supporting terms and question forms.

The strongest public search-language evidence is concentrated around:

- thermal interface material suppliers / manufacturers
- thermal pad manufacturers in China
- thermal pad compression ratio and compression set
- thermal pad dielectric constant / electrically insulating thermal pad
- thermal conductivity vs thermal resistance
- thermal pad vs thermal grease vs thermal gel
- thermal pad selection guide
- what is thermal gel

Highly specific phrases involving OBC second source, 800G sourcing, PCS gel suppliers and battery cooling-plate samples remain commercially valuable, but their demand is not proven by Autocomplete. They are Tier B supporting opportunities rather than Tier A search-demand claims.
