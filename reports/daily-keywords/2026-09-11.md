## Executive decision

The next content cycle should not target broad consumer queries such as “thermal pad for CPU” or “what is thermal paste.” Public Google Autocomplete strongly favors those broad terms, but their B2B conversion fit is weak. The better OUYANG THERMAL opportunity is a narrower set of application, qualification, benchmark and supplier queries where one qualified visit can be commercially meaningful even when exact demand is unavailable.

The existing site already covers foundational selection questions well. The largest commercial gaps are second-source qualification, application-specific supplier discovery, benchmark planning, RFQ preparation and reliability validation.

## Evidence used

- Google Autocomplete was queried directly in English for 30 seeds covering supplier, manufacturer, alternative, application, failure, validation and material-property intent.
- Public search results were reviewed for 800G/1.6T optical modules, battery-pack thermal gel, EV/OBC/DC-DC applications, thermal potting and structural adhesive terminology.
- All 30 existing OUYANG THERMAL article titles and URLs were checked for primary-intent overlap.
- No monthly search volume was invented. Google Trends, Keyword Planner and Search Console query data were not available in this run.

## Recommended first three — for review only

These three follow the required daily commercial mix but no article has been written:

1. **Engineering problem:** `thermal gel pump out reliability test for OBC` — score 90. Funnel position: diagnosis → validation → benchmark.
2. **Application + selection:** `how to select thermal pads for 800G optical modules` — score 93. Funnel position: design selection → application discussion → sample.
3. **Procurement / second source:** `OBC thermal gel second source qualification` — score 96. Funnel position: supplier evaluation → qualification → RFQ.

## Cannibalization decisions

- Do not create another generic “thermal pad supplier China” page. Optimize `/thermal-pad-supplier-china/` when Search Console data becomes available.
- Do not create another generic OBC TIM overview. Use `/obc/thermal-interface-materials-for-obc-complete-selection-guide/` as the cluster hub.
- Keep the SP2000 alternative page product-specific; a future second-source qualification guide must target process and validation intent rather than the same product query.
- A future 800G page must address 800G/OSFP interface constraints and link to the existing general optical-module pad selection page.
- A future OBC pump-out validation page must focus on test design and acceptance logic, not repeat the existing explanation of why pump-out occurs.
