## Applications supported

Relevant applications include OBC and DC/DC assemblies, PCS and ESS power electronics, battery PACK cooling plates, BMS electronics, and industrial power modules. Each has different requirements for gap filling, vertical stability, electrical isolation, dispensing, service temperature, and long-term movement.

## Key engineering requirements

Define gap range, interface area, heat load, target temperature, effective thermal resistance, electrical requirements, dispense path, cure condition for two-part materials, density, pump-out risk, vibration, thermal cycling, and rework expectations. Conductivity values are only comparable when methods and conditions are understood.

## Evaluation and qualification

Screen documents first, then confirm dispense repeatability and achievable BLT. Use representative hardware for thermal and reliability tests. Record material lot, mix ratio, cure, pressure, temperatures, and aging. For second-source work, compare the candidate with the incumbent under identical conditions rather than claiming equivalence from datasheets.
